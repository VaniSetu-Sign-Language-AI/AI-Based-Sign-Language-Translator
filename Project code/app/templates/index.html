How it works

From camera to sentence in three moves

Step 1

Collect

Record webcam frames or drop in labelled image folders. Each frame becomes 63 wrist-normalised landmarks in landmarks.csv.

Step 2

Train

Stage one pre-trains the CNN–LSTM backbone; stage two fits a RandomForest on its frozen embeddings. Re-fit just the forest with --rf-only.

Step 3

Predict

Capture a sign for instant transcription with confidence smoothing — or type text to preview its signs and speak the result aloud.
